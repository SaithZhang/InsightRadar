# InsightRadar Product Benchmark

This benchmark keeps product iteration tied to durable user workflows rather than visual feature copying. Recheck the official product pages before each major product sprint because vendor capabilities change.

| Leading product | Public workflow strength | InsightRadar response |
|---|---|---|
| Meet Kevin Alpha Report | Daily market-open cadence, overnight world events, fresh research, trade ideas, lines to watch, and archive | Borrow the concise habit and conclusion-first delivery; do not substitute creator authority or generic calls for portfolio evidence and calibration. |
| Klarion | Portfolio risk analysis, market-move alerts, exposure mapping, stress tests, confidence, and historical context | Make every promoted event answer what changed, why it matters to this user, and whether it changes a plan or risk state. |
| AlphaSense | Unified search, deep research, monitoring, alerts, and scheduled workflow agents | Keep research evidence auditable, then push only portfolio-relevant changes into the daily decision surface. |
| Bloomberg PORT | Portfolio performance attribution, risk analytics, scenario analysis, and automated reporting | Add outcome and attribution layers after action generation; do not treat a recommendation as complete until its later result is measurable. |
| Koyfin | Portfolio P/L, exposure, contribution, customizable reports, watchlists, and alerts | Preserve simple portfolio import and conclusion-first reporting, then add benchmark/exposure contribution after the signal ledger has enough history. |
| TradingView | Historical strategy testing connected to server-side realtime strategy alerts | Separate historical evaluation from live conditional actions and never score a signal before its horizon matures. |

No single reference is the product blueprint. InsightRadar combines a concise Alpha delivery habit, portfolio-aware relevance, auditable research monitoring, explicit triggers, and outcome calibration with A-share-native filings, policy, market-structure, and risk evidence.

## Current Product Gap Order

1. Signal outcome ledger: recommendation -> later return -> hit/miss -> MFE/MAE -> calibrated product score.
2. Portfolio/benchmark contribution: explain what actually drove return and concentration risk.
3. Outcome-calibrated alerts: promote only evidence changes and triggers whose historical sample is visible.
4. Scenario stress tests: translate macro, sector, and single-name shocks into portfolio impact.

## Acceptance Rule

Every benchmark-driven feature must add one verifiable product behavior, produce a fresh artifact from a real CLI run, preserve explicit data gaps, and update `feature_list.json` plus `progress.md` with evidence.
