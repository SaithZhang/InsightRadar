## Admission Criteria

A V3.1 item may start only when:

1. No other product increment is active, unless the owner explicitly reprioritizes and parks it.
2. The problem is reproducible and mapped to a user decision or risk outcome, or the owner has explicitly selected the bounded version goal.
3. The change preserves the four-task information architecture and rule/user/AI/trade authority boundary.
4. Real data, source freshness, counter-evidence, rollback behavior, and unavailable states are specified.
5. Acceptance evidence, a kill criterion, and proportional verification exist.
6. The delta is selected in `CURRENT_STATE.md` and its status/evidence is recorded before the next increment starts.

V3.2, a fifth first-level task, cloud/multi-user delivery, and automatic execution are not authorized by opening V3.1.
