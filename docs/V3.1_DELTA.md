## Admission Criteria

A V3.1 item may start only when:

1. The ten-run pilot is complete.
2. The observed problem is reproducible and mapped to a user decision or risk outcome.
3. The change preserves the four-task information architecture.
4. Real data, source freshness, counter-evidence, and rollback behavior are specified.
5. A kill criterion and proportional verification plan exist.
6. The owner explicitly authorizes the delta.

Ordinary experience feedback remains logged during the pilot but is not implemented immediately.
