## Durable Constraints

- Use `InsightRadar` for the repository, Codex project, automation, and user-facing product context. Keep `stock_assist` only as the internal Python package and retain legacy CLI aliases for compatibility.
- One feature at a time unless the user explicitly changes priority.
- A feature is not complete without executed verification and recorded evidence.
- Generated JSON, Markdown, and HTML that share a report contract must be refreshed together.
- Private runtime data stays ignored; examples, schemas, and reproducible configuration stay version controlled.
- The newest explicit user-confirmed `data/portfolio.json` snapshot takes precedence over an older manual broker TSV. Missing broker fields stay null and render as `未提供`; they must never be converted to zero to improve apparent coverage.
- Core reliability reports distinguish structural action coverage from strict decision-ready coverage. A baseline may pass by measuring and exposing a real gap, but the product must not call that holding decision-ready until the gap is closed.
- Holding-management consent, data quality, and historical entry context are separate. Missing/stale consent produces a deterministic system proposal and does not block base analysis. `context_status` records proposal/confirmation state; `data_status` independently blocks only capabilities that depend on invalid account or market data, and user confirmation cannot clear it. Every data blocker has a plan-linked repair contract; provider/source faults use version-bound system retry, broker snapshot gaps use the approved import flow, and failed repair remains blocked. Daily-series price-basis/mapping repair may select a separately recorded Tencent forward-adjusted whole-series fallback, never stitched with the primary provider and always retaining the primary fault in provenance. Unknown entry-time history stays visible as a Review-quality gap and cannot be reconstructed with hindsight or AI. See ADR-0016.
- Failed research validation remains diagnostic and cannot silently become a production signal.
- State-team monitoring may prove an ETF-unit exit lower bound from dated holdings and current total units, but must not relabel that lower bound as cash net selling, underlying-stock liquidation, or complete 2015 rescue-book coverage.
- Short-cycle ETF total-share changes may tighten or loosen that lower bound, but they cannot identify the current seller; mixed 5/20-observation structures must remain visible instead of being flattened into a single liquidation narrative.
- The next-session decision surface must merge the latest risk, market/state-team, industry, and holding evidence into explicit opening/upside/flat/downside branches. Missing or stale risk inputs fail closed on new exposure, while unknown broker fields stay unknown and do not erase a live holding from the dashboard.
- Market-regime gauges must keep their units, as-of date, ingredients, and calibration state visible. Bear-bull, fear-greed, and crowding are separate diagnostics; none independently authorizes a trade, and fewer than 20 crowding snapshots cannot be presented as a historical percentile.
- The bear-bull score is a persisted close-finalized state machine, not a free-running narrative gauge. Formal and candidate values remain separate; daily change is capped, same-day rules are deduplicated, support failure uses multi-bar confirmation, and hard-risk vetoes can block a risk-budget upgrade even when a candidate score improves. Every change or block keeps rule, threshold, observed value, evidence source/as-of, explanation, and calibration state.
- Market levels are decision inputs rather than decorative annotations. Support testing, confirmed support failure, repair, and breakout states must have explicit stance/risk-budget effects, invalidation logic, and signed distance from the current level.
- Fixed-anchor market breadth must use a declared anchor date, listing-date eligibility, provider-adjusted interval returns, and visible coverage. Equal-weight and median-stock equivalent points are diagnostics rather than official index levels; incomplete coverage cannot validate viral stock-count claims.
- Iwencai completed-close futures basis must resolve one shared date for CSI 300/SSE 50/CSI 500/CSI 1000, join only CFFEX rows from that exact date, reject zero-open-interest expired contracts, and discover the nearest configured contracts from provider codes. It may expose volume/open interest and available daily change, but without a real prior intraday observation it cannot invent a four-minute basis change or authorize an action. A stale close during the live session must fall through to serial AmazingData or an explicit gap.
- Broker import is a loopback-only preview/approval workflow, not a static browser save. It may not upload data, accept a browser-selected destination, or silently overwrite project data. Canonical `portfolio.json` and risk-profile replacement require explicit approval, timestamped backups, atomic writes, rollback on failed refresh, and a sequential rerun before the decision report changes.
- Broker import does not authorize guessing a position's beta class or treating a zero broker weight as known zero. After approval, a deterministic `portfolio-beta` step uses `000300.SH`, a 120-session simple-return window, at least 60 observations, and a 1.20 threshold; it persists beta/R²/source/as-of/quality evidence before `risk-watch`. Missing weights and non-ready beta evidence stay unknown, block reconciliation, and keep strict decision-ready coverage at zero.
- Percentage trim guidance becomes an executable quantity only when shares/availability are known; board-lot flooring may correctly produce zero and must then expose the requested percentage and a manual choice rather than manufacturing a sell order.
- Style rotation uses fixed transparent ETF/index proxies and requires relative strength, breadth, turnover/fund evidence, and persistence across 5/20/60-session horizons. A single indicator, approximate `close * volume` turnover, missing earnings evidence, or a short-lived seesaw cannot confirm a main-line switch or override the risk budget.
- Product-scope or module-ring changes require `docs/product-charter.md`, an ADR, and a refreshed `CURRENT_STATE.md`.
